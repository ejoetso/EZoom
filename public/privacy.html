EZoom Privacy Notice

Last updated: 14 October 2026

EZoom is operated by Ejoe Tso. For privacy questions, contact eozoe2025@gmail.com.

Information used

Educator registration uses a name, email address, and password. The service stores a password hash, not the original password. Students provide a name and email address to join a classroom. The classroom processes messages, poll responses, whiteboard activity, and live audio/video or screen content when those features are used.

Purpose and sharing

This information is used to provide accounts, classroom access, and live teaching features. If an educator chooses an AI assistance feature, relevant classroom text is sent to the configured Google Gemini service to generate a summary or quiz. Do not enter sensitive information into an AI request without permission from the people involved.

Camera, microphone, and recording

Camera and microphone access is requested only when a classroom feature needs it. Device permissions can be changed in system settings. Classroom recordings are initiated by the user and handled on their device; participants should be notified and consent obtained where required.

Retention and account deletion

Registered educators can delete their account inside the app from the educator account panel. This removes the saved account record and invalidates active sign-in sessions. Classroom sessions are held in server memory while active. An operator of a self-hosted deployment controls its own server storage, backups, and retention.

Contact

For access, privacy, or deletion questions, email eozoe2025@gmail.com.
